Calibration and legal limits

Phone microphones aren't calibrated. Hold the phone beside a real sound level meter and adjust the offset until both match. Limits are Ghana EPA ambient levels in dB(A), day (6am–10pm) / night.

A citizen science project. Readings stay on this phone until you export them; one person can import everyone's files to build the shared map. Phone readings are indicative, so follow up hotspots with certified meters. Map © OpenStreetMap contributors.
